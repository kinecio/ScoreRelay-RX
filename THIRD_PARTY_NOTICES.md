## Fabric.js

`public/vendor/fabric.min.js` bundles [Fabric.js](http://fabricjs.com/)
(MIT License), used by the graphics designer (`/designer`) and the live
output player (`/output/:id`) to render and edit canvas-based graphics in
the browser. Full license text: `public/vendor/fabric.LICENSE.txt`.

The desktop apps additionally bundle Electron (MIT; its Chromium notices ship inside the app as
LICENSES.chromium.html) and `@stoprocent/noble` (MIT) for Bluetooth.
